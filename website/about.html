// OUR STORY

Our story

Quality creates trust. Trust builds lasting relationships.

SSG began in 2021 with a simple idea: the Northeast deserves infrastructure built for its roads, its weather and its communities.

Today we deliver solar and AC street lighting, high masts, National Flag poles, water purification and outdoor fitness equipment to panchayats, institutions, industries and government departments.

Every project follows one rule: get it right on site, and make it last.

// OUR MISSION

To be a trusted leader in sustainable infrastructure, delivering high-quality solutions that create long-term value for our customers and communities.

// OUR VISION

To keep innovating without compromising on quality, and to build lasting relationships through reliable products, timely execution and exceptional service.
